Only the six mmiron sample regions were extracted from the SoundFont. No code,
other instruments, or recordings by other creators were copied.
The mirror has downmixed to mono PCM, aligned attacks, normalized loudness,
and faded tails. HCMAI wraps this PCM in 16-bit, 44.1 kHz WAV files.

The player sequences and layers these recorded strokes into basic thekas:
Na = na, Tin/Di = naopen, Tu = tun, Ge/Ga = ghe,
Ta/Ti/Ra = te, Ki/Ka/Kat = ke.
Dha layers na + ghe; Dhin/Dhi layer naopen + ghe.
These are a limited-kit practice arrangement, not recordings of a musician
performing each complete taal or a full set of distinct bol articulations.
Dhamar and Chautal are tabla-kit adaptations of pakhawaj-associated taals.
Changing tempo changes stroke spacing, not the pitch of the recordings.
